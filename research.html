Research scope

Research areas

Research has examined economic and health outcomes across healthcare topics, including treatments for liver disease, transplantation, and infectious diseases. Methods such as economic evaluation and real-world data analysis are selected to suit each research question.

Health economics and economic evaluation
We assess the value of medicines and health technologies by comparing costs and health outcomes.
Outcomes research
We analyze patient health outcomes and healthcare use to generate evidence for clinical practice and policy decisions.
Real-world data
We analyze healthcare use and outcomes using data from routine clinical practice.
Drug pricing, reimbursement, and pharmaceutical policy
We study the effects of medicine use and healthcare policy.

Methods

Methods

QALYQuality-adjusted life-year
We compare the health outcomes of treatments by considering both survival and health-related quality of life.
ICERIncremental cost-effectiveness ratio
We analyze and compare the additional cost required to achieve an additional health benefit.
Markov model
We model long-term transitions between health states, costs, and outcomes.
RWDReal-world data
Observations of healthcare use and outcomes in practice complement the evidence for healthcare decisions.
