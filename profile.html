About

Hye-Lin Kim

I'm an Associate Professor in the College of Pharmacy at Sahmyook University, where I research and teach social and administrative pharmacy. I joined the university in March 2016 and study scientific evidence for healthcare decision-making in the Social Pharmacy Laboratory.

Research scope

Research interests

My health economics and outcomes research has covered treatments for liver disease, transplantation, and infectious diseases. Methods include cost-effectiveness analysis, cost-utility analysis using QALYs and ICERs, Markov models, and outcomes analysis using real-world data.
