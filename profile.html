Biography

Research and teaching

The laboratory produces scientific evidence for healthcare decision-making: what a treatment costs, what health it returns, and how that evidence reaches the people who allocate budgets. Hye-Lin Kim joined Sahmyook University in March 2016.

Research scope

Research interests

My health economics and outcomes research has covered treatments for liver disease, transplantation, and infectious diseases. Methods include cost-effectiveness analysis, cost-utility analysis using QALYs and ICERs, Markov models, and outcomes analysis using real-world data.
