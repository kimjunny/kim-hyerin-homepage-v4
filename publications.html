Evaluating treatment response thresholds for cost-effective treatment in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease

Treatment response thresholds for cost-effective MASLD therapy

A 20-year cost-utility model assessed the response needed for a hypothetical MASLD treatment, assumed to cost $20,000 annually, to be cost-effective. The results apply only to the specified price and assumptions.

Full text at the publisher DOI 10.3350/cmh.2025.0796 PubMed · PMID 41183809

2021Gut

Markov model · Gut

Cost-effectiveness of antiviral treatment in immune-tolerant chronic hepatitis B

Economic evaluation of treatment timing in chronic hepatitis B

A simulated cohort of 10,000 people and a 20-year Markov model were used to compare costs and QALYs for immediate versus delayed treatment. The estimates depend on the model inputs and assumptions.

DOI 10.1136/gutjnl-2020-321309 PubMed · PMID 33239344
Interpretation

Interpreting the results

Economic evaluation results vary with the study population, comparator strategies, prices, time horizon, and model assumptions. The scope and assumptions of each study must therefore be considered when interpreting its findings for policy or clinical outcomes.
